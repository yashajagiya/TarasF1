The Meaning of Taras (तरस्)

In classical Sanskrit, "Taras" (तरस्) translates to speed, velocity, dynamic energy, impetus, and strength. It encapsulates the pure soul of motorsport: lightning reflexes, supreme aerodynamics, and tireless pursuit of performance.

👨‍💻 Developer & Philosophy

Taras is designed and built by Yash Ajagiya, an independent Android engineer and Formula 1 fan from India.

Taras was born to reject bloated, ad-infested sports apps. Instead, Taras provides a clean, elegant, Google-inspired Material 3 aesthetic, zero advertising, zero accounts, and 100% on-device private data storage.

🌟 Core Design Pillars

🔒 100% Private

No account sign-up, no analytics trackers, no ad networks. All preferences live strictly on your device.

🇮🇳 Indian Legal Adherence

Built compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and IT Act 2000.

⚡ Modern & Open Source

Powered by Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore, and Navigation 3 under the Apache 2.0 license.

📱 Key Features

  • The Paddock: Real-time motorsport news parsed from public RSS feeds using Ksoup.
  • The Grid: 2026 driver & constructor standings, detailed career statistics, and head-to-head comparison tool.
  • Race Weekend Calendar: Automatic countdown timers to FP1, FP2, FP3, Qualifying, Sprint, and Grand Prix.
  • F1 GridTCG 2026: Free, offline digital trading cards with scratch-to-reveal packs and battle arena.
  • Glance Widgets: Beautiful Android home-screen countdown and standings widgets.
